# Dublin Pubs - The Impossible Dream

Myself and my brother have a goal to drink in every pub in Dublin. The site keeps our visits and reviews alongside the current Revenue licence list.

## Site

[Dublin Pubs Map](https://irishshagua.github.io/dublin-pubs-map)

The map shows visited pubs and their reviews. The total is the number of current seven-day ordinary publican licences in Revenue's Dublin city and county register; it is a licence count, not necessarily a count of distinct pubs. It is separate from the historical visit total.

The site reads the visit list and the generated `remaining-pubs.json` licence snapshot from `master`. The experimental matching and routing investigation is documented in the main branch README; it is not used on the website.

The template used for the site is from [StartBootstrap](https://github.com/BlackrockDigital/startbootstrap-stylish-portfolio).
